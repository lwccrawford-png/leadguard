# HVAC Sales Playbooks

## Positioning

Primary offer:

Your HVAC business's AI front office and revenue recovery team.

Outcome promise:

Answer more calls, book more jobs, recover missed opportunities, follow up every estimate, and show the owner what revenue the system influenced.

Avoid leading with:

- Chatbot
- AI widget
- Automation setup
- Generic virtual receptionist

Lead with:

- Missed-call recovery
- After-hours booking
- Estimate follow-up
- Membership growth
- Replacement opportunity capture
- Owner reporting

## Ideal Customer Profile

Best-fit HVAC companies:

- 5-50 technicians.
- Residential service/replacement or mixed residential-light commercial.
- Meaningful call volume.
- Missed calls, after-hours demand, or front desk overload.
- Uses a field service CRM or calendar system.
- Has average repair/replacement tickets large enough to justify recurring spend.
- Owner or GM cares about revenue leakage.

Poor-fit customers:

- No consistent inbound demand.
- No scheduling discipline.
- No CRM or willingness to centralize records.
- Wants AI to replace licensed technical judgment.
- Refuses consent/compliance requirements.

## Discovery Questions

- How many inbound calls do you get per day in peak season?
- How many are missed, abandoned, or go to voicemail?
- What happens after hours?
- How fast do web leads get a response?
- How many open estimates are never followed up?
- What is your average repair ticket?
- What is your average replacement ticket?
- What percentage of customers are maintenance members?
- Which CRM or dispatch tool do you use?
- Where does the front desk lose the most time?

## ROI Hooks

Missed calls:

If the company misses 20 calls per month and only 25% would have booked, that is 5 jobs. At a configured average ticket of $X, the recovered opportunity is 5 * $X before replacement upside.

Estimate follow-up:

If 40 open estimates per month receive inconsistent follow-up and AI revives even 2-3, the platform can justify itself quickly.

Membership:

If the AI converts completed repairs into memberships, the business gains repeat revenue, seasonal schedule control, and retention.

Replacement:

One captured replacement consultation can cover multiple months of subscription depending on the market.

## Objection Handling For Buyers

"We already have someone answering phones."

Response: Great. This helps them catch overflow, after-hours, repetitive questions, and follow-up work so staff can focus on live customers and high-value issues.

"I don't want AI diagnosing systems."

Response: It should not. The pack is designed for safe triage, scheduling, education, and escalation. Technical diagnosis stays with licensed technicians.

"Will customers know it is AI?"

Response: Configure disclosure according to your policy. The more important point is that it should be useful, safe, and easy to reach a human when needed.

"Our CRM is messy."

Response: The first deployment phase includes field mapping, required data cleanup, and a controlled pilot before full automation.
