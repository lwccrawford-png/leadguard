# LeadGuard GTM And BIP Priority

Created: 2026-08-01

## Current Product Reality

LeadGuard is strongest today as a managed AI front-desk and lead-qualification widget for small businesses that:

- Receive meaningful website inquiries.
- Need fast answers to common questions.
- Want visitors pushed toward scheduling or direct follow-up.
- Do not want to configure software themselves.
- Are not already using a serious CRM/help desk.
- Can justify the monthly fee from one captured or better-qualified opportunity.

The product should not be positioned as a full AI operating system yet. V1 does not have live booking, live CRM writeback beyond the internal lead board, phone answering, true multi-tenant SaaS, or deep vertical workflow automation. Those are future upgrades. The current GTM should sell a practical managed service:

> LeadGuard answers website visitors, qualifies them, and makes sure good leads do not disappear.

## Positioning

Primary positioning:

**AI front desk for small business websites**

Sharper vertical version:

**A managed AI front desk that turns website visitors into booked calls, quote requests, and follow-up-ready leads.**

Avoid leading with:

- AI chatbot
- Generic website widget
- AI agency
- Workflow automation platform
- CRM replacement

Lead with:

- Instant website response
- Better-qualified leads
- Fewer missed opportunities
- Faster setup
- Done-for-you configuration
- Simple monthly service
- Owner-friendly lead board

## Pricing Recommendation

The current $99/month + $399 setup target is useful as an entry point, but likely underprices higher-value verticals such as HVAC, roofing, med spas, and credit repair.

Recommended validation ladder:

| Offer | Monthly | Setup | Who It Fits |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Starter Pilot | $99 | $399 | Early proof, friendly local businesses, low-risk validation |
| Vertical Front Desk | $149-$199 | $599-$799 | HVAC, auto repair, credit repair, med spa, quote/appointment businesses |
| Priority Setup | $249-$299 | $999-$1,299 | Higher-value verticals with stronger lead economics and more handoff customization |

Do not publish all tiers yet. Test them in sales conversations. If HVAC prospects accept $199/month without resistance, do not anchor the category at $99.

## First 5 BIPs To Build

### 1. HVAC / Home Comfort

Why first:

- High average ticket.
- Clear pain: urgent no-cooling/no-heat inquiries, quote requests, maintenance questions.
- Strong ROI story: one booked repair or replacement consult can justify months of service.
- Reusable base for plumbing, electrical, garage doors, roofing, and restoration.

Main LeadGuard fit:

- Website chat qualification.
- Safe triage.
- Scheduling link handoff.
- Quote/diagnostic request capture.
- FAQ coverage.

Risk:

- Many urgent jobs still come by phone, not chat. Position as website lead capture and after-hours web response, not full call answering.

### 2. Roofing / Exterior Contractors

Why:

- High-ticket quote requests.
- Storm-season lead spikes.
- Visitors need simple answers before requesting an estimate.
- Business owners understand the value of one captured job.

Main LeadGuard fit:

- Estimate request qualification.
- Insurance/storm-damage FAQ boundaries.
- Scheduling link or callback capture.

Risk:

- Avoid claims about insurance coverage or claim approval.

### 3. Med Spa / Aesthetic Clinics

Why:

- High lifetime value.
- Consultations matter.
- Many website visitors are comparison shopping.
- Front desk staff handle repetitive treatment, pricing, financing, and booking questions.

Main LeadGuard fit:

- Consultation booking.
- Treatment FAQ routing.
- Soft qualification.
- Hand-off for medical/provider-specific questions.

Risk:

- Must avoid medical advice, diagnosis, treatment guarantees, or exact pricing unless configured.

### 4. Auto Repair / Specialty Automotive Service

Why:

- High volume of practical service questions.
- Visitors ask about symptoms, appointments, pricing, warranties, and timelines.
- User has automotive domain familiarity.
- Works well for specialty shops, diesel repair, transmission, auto glass, detailing, and performance shops.

Main LeadGuard fit:

- Appointment request qualification.
- Basic service FAQs.
- Estimate/callback capture.

Risk:

- Lower average tickets for some shops. Prioritize specialty repair and shops with strong web traffic.

### 5. Credit Repair / Professional Financial Services

Why:

- Existing demo proof.
- Strong need for careful qualification and compliance boundaries.
- Prospects often need education before booking.
- User has domain credibility.

Main LeadGuard fit:

- Consultation booking.
- Lead qualification.
- Compliance-aware FAQs.
- Clear handoff to human advisor.

Risk:

- Must avoid guarantees, timelines, legal/financial advice, and case-specific recommendations.

## GTM Path From One Pilot To Repeatable Sales

### Phase 1: Validate Paid Conversion

Goal: Convert 3-5 paying customers, not maximize volume.

Actions:

- Convert LMTLSS from free pilot to a paid pilot or paid support plan if appropriate.
- Use Evolve as proof that LeadGuard adapts across verticals.
- Sell 5 founder slots with hands-on setup.
- Offer a clear 30-day success checkpoint.
- Track outcomes manually: conversations, leads captured, bookings clicked, unresolved questions, owner feedback.

Suggested founder offer:

> I'll install a managed AI front desk on your website, configure it around your business, and tune it for 30 days. If it does not produce qualified conversations or reduce repetitive questions, you can cancel before month two.

### Phase 2: Verticalize The Sales Motion

Goal: Pick one lead vertical after initial response data.

Use HVAC first unless real-world conversations show stronger pull from credit repair or another network-driven niche.

Create for each priority vertical:

- One demo page.
- One 60-second walkthrough.
- One BIP script.
- One facts template.
- One FAQ template.
- One outreach message.
- One ROI example.

### Phase 3: Repeatable Outreach

Daily motion:

- Identify 20 local businesses in one vertical.
- Check if their website has slow/no chat, weak FAQ, or poor contact flow.
- Generate a side-by-side demo using the current demo generator.
- Send a direct message framed around their actual website.
- Offer a short setup review, not a generic SaaS pitch.

Simple outreach angle:

> I made a quick AI front-desk demo for your website. It answers common visitor questions, qualifies service requests, and sends ready-to-follow-up leads to your team. Want me to send the preview?

### Phase 4: Pricing Validation

Test pricing in sequence:

1. $99/month + $399 setup for warm/friendly validation.
2. $149/month + $599 setup for HVAC/auto/credit repair.
3. $199/month + $799 setup for higher-intent prospects.
4. $249/month + $999 setup when the prospect wants custom FAQs, more careful scripting, or extra handoff setup.

The goal is not to defend $99. The goal is to find the highest price that still feels like an easy yes compared with one missed opportunity.

## Recommended BIP Format

Keep the current LeadGuard drop-in shape:

- Flow script
- Facts
- FAQs

But store each BIP with optional implementation notes:

- Best-fit buyer
- What not to promise
- High-value lead signals
- Suggested pipeline stages
- Suggested demo scenario

This is better than only creating script/fact/FAQ templates because it gives the managed-service operator repeatable judgment without requiring any code or product redesign.
